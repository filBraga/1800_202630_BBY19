Stop Doom

Put down the endless feed and pick up a puzzle. Stop Doom helps you swap doomscrolling for a few minutes of Sudoku every day.

Get started

What is Stop Doom?

"Doomscrolling" is when you keep scrolling through social media or news for a long time, even when it makes you feel tired, anxious or bored. Most of us do it without noticing.

Stop Doom is a web app made by a team of BCIT students. When you feel like scrolling, open Stop Doom instead and solve a Sudoku puzzle. The app keeps track of how much time you spend on puzzles and how many days in a row you play, so you can see your progress.

How it works

1. Play a puzzle

Choose a difficulty (easy, medium or hard) and solve a Sudoku. You can use pencil mode to write small notes in the cells.

2. Track your time

A timer saves how long you play. You can see your puzzle history and the total time you spent on puzzles instead of scrolling.

3. Keep your streak

Play every day to grow your daily streak. Add friends and get reminders and motivation messages to keep going.

Our goals

  • Less screen time on social media. Help people notice and reduce the time they lose to doomscrolling.
  • Better mental health. Replace stressful feeds with a calm activity that gives you a small win.
  • Stronger problem-solving skills. Puzzles train your focus, memory and logic.
  • Build a healthy habit. Streaks, reminders and friends make it easier to come back every day.
